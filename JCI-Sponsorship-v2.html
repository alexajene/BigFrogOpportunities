Not only is it my intention to bring great match-ups to the fans, it is also to build a professional platform that supports grappling athletes, brings people together, and helps grow the Jiu-Jitsu community both here locally and across the country.

About The JCI
Founded by 5th-degree Pedro Sauer Black Belt Jeff Curran, the Invitational highlights the integrity, artistry, and excitement of Jiu-Jitsu.
Every event has sold out. Backed by elite gyms and athletes — over 33 clubs from across the U.S. represented.
Live-streamed worldwide on YouTube. SuckerPunch Entertainment — the most trusted name in athlete management — is a proud collaborator.

High-Impact Brand Visibility

From on-mat logos and livestream branding to event reels and social media campaigns, your company will be in front of both local and national audiences, amplified by press coverage and media partnerships.

Massive Reach & Engagement

JCI 3 delivered 1.77M combined impressions — up +93% from JCI 2, nearly tripling reach from JCI 1. Supported by influencers, UFC veterans, and top coaches.

Targeted Audience in a Growing Market

The Jiu-Jitsu community is expanding worldwide — athletes, fitness enthusiasts, and families who value health, performance, and loyalty to brands that support their sport.

Association with Integrity & Prestige

JCI embodies professionalism and the principle of "Athletes First. Always." Aligning your brand with this reputation strengthens your credibility within the martial arts community and the wider health and wellness industry.
